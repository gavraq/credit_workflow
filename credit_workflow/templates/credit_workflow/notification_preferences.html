Information

Configure how and when you receive notifications about activity in the Credit Risk Workflow application.

Email Notifications

Email notifications are sent to your registered email address. Make sure your email is up-to-date in your profile settings.

In-App Notifications

In-app notifications appear in the notification dropdown in the top navigation bar and in the Notification Center.

Daily Digest

When enabled, you'll receive a single digest email containing all notifications for the day, instead of individual emails for each notification.

Need Help?

If you're having issues with your notification settings or not receiving expected notifications, please contact the support team.
